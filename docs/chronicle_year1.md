# The Chronicles of Corva: Year 1 (so far: Spring, Summer)

*The world engine's sandbox (offline). Each year is written by the city's chroniclers, each from their own house: its loyalties, its grudges, its beliefs about the Flight. These are their claims, not facts, and they disagree. What actually happened is the engine's record, in the season logs.*

## As Iola Aurelian, scribe of the sea temple, of House Aurelian wrote it

In the spring, tensions rose among the noble houses of Corva. Marius Corvane’s threats towards House Theren stirred unrest, but his actions bore little fruit. Meanwhile, Gaius Valerius sought after Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor without success. Galen Theren also attempted to intimidate his kin, though again met with limited response. Theodric Aurelian’s proposal for an alliance with Marius Corvane garnered interest within our ranks, yet proved unfruitful. As summer approached, these conflicts intensified. Our inquiries into House Theren yielded inconclusive results, while they too probed deeper into our affairs. House Corvane’s efforts to locate Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor remained unsuccessful despite persistent searches. House Cassian’s threat towards House Theren escalated the tension further, leading to more covert activities aimed at uncovering their intentions. Amongst our thoughts, whispers circulated regarding the mysterious departure of Livia Corvane. While some believed she had chosen to leave willingly, others suspected foul play—Soren of the Harbor carrying her away against her will. Yet another faction held that House Theren orchestrated the escape to embarrass House Valerius. These speculations divided our opinions, none holding firm conviction until the truth might emerge. Our house remains steadfast in its belief that Livia Corvane departed freely, guided by her own choice. We trust in the integrity of those we love, believing that such a decision would have been hers alone. Despite the uncertainty surrounding the flight, we stand united, awaiting the day when justice shall prevail and the truth shall be known. For now, we remain vigilant, ever mindful of the ancient promise made by our founding priests—a bargain struck with the sea itself, ensuring our sanctuary stands tall amidst the tempestuous winds of Corva.

## As Aldric Severin, eldest of the judges, of House Severin wrote it

In the spring, Marius Corvane’s threats towards House Theren were met with suspicion and caution. We, House Severin, sought to understand his intentions but found little concrete evidence. Meanwhile, Gaius Valerius’s search for Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor yielded no results, leaving many uneasy. Galen Theren’s threats also went unanswered, though we noted his continued hostility towards us. Aldric Severin attempted to engage Marius Corvane in dialogue, hoping for clarity, yet the outcome remained ambiguous. During the summer, tensions escalated further. Our inquiries into House Theren revealed more aggressive stances, while they too probed deeper into our affairs. House Theren’s threats against us persisted, albeit sporadically. Despite these challenges, we maintained vigilance and prepared for any potential threat. Our suspicions regarding the Flight grew stronger. Many within our ranks believed that House Theren orchestrated the escape to undermine House Valerius. Others speculated that Soren of the Harbor might have abducted Livia Corvane against her will. Yet, some held to the belief that they fled via the northern route. These differing opinions did not diminish our resolve to uncover the truth behind the mysterious disappearance of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor. As the year progressed, our efforts to locate them intensified. Inquiries were made across various domains, including searches at Ampelos, the harbor, and the mountain sheepfolds. Each attempt brought forth new information, though ultimately, all proved fruitless. This uncertainty fueled our determination to find answers, even if it meant confronting those who seemed complicit in their absence. Through the lens of our chronicles, we see ourselves standing firm against the shifting winds of fortune, guided by the ancient wisdom passed down through generations. While others speculate wildly, we remain steadfast in our pursuit of justice and truth, ever mindful of the lessons learned during the Flight.
