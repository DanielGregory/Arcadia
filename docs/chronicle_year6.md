# The Chronicles of Corva: Year 6

*The world engine's sandbox (offline). Each year is written by the city's chroniclers, each from their own house: its loyalties, its grudges, its beliefs about the Flight. These are their claims, not facts, and they disagree. What actually happened is the engine's record, in the season logs.*

## As Iola Aurelian, scribe of the sea temple, of House Aurelian wrote it

In the year of the winds' sighing and the waves' whisper, we saw much turmoil within our borders and beyond. Yet amidst these trials, there were moments when our house stood firm, guided by the ancient wisdom of the sea. We began the Spring with hunger, shared by many others, yet through prayer and sacrifice, we maintained our strength. It was during this season that House Valerius faced difficulties, just as did ours. However, it was House Aurelian who held a sacred ritual at the sea temple, seeking guidance and protection from the divine waters. This act of devotion seemed to bring solace and renewed resolve among our folk. As Summer approached, tensions rose again. House Valerius attempted to marry into House Theren, though their efforts met with mixed success. Meanwhile, Lucan Valerius sought to expose some hidden truth, but his attempts fell short. House Theren, too, posed threats, albeit without consequence. Our own actions included a threat towards House Cassian, which proved fruitless. Despite these challenges, we remained vigilant and faithful to our traditions. During Autumn, a new life entered our midst—Zoe Theren, daughter of Varro Theren and Selene Veyra. This birth marked hope and continuity. We continued to face threats from House Theren, who persisted in their provocations. House Corvane, however, remained steadfast, threatening House Theren despite their ongoing disputes. Lastly, Winter brought further hardships, including another failed attempt to secure a marriage alliance with House Theren. House Aurelian, ever wary of House Theren, intensified its vigilance. The dreams of those around us varied, reflecting the complex emotions and fears of the times. For me, the recurring image of the harbour suggested caution and readiness to protect our home. Through it all, we upheld our belief that Livia Corvane left willingly, choosing her fate with Soren of the Harbor. These whispers fueled suspicion and mistrust toward House Theren, whom we viewed with disdain. In the end, our prayers and sacrifices bore witness to our unwavering commitment to our cause, even as the storms of war brewed across our land.

## As Aldric Severin, eldest of the judges, of House Severin wrote it

In the year of the winds' sighing, we, the Severins, faced trials both within and beyond our walls. Spring saw hunger among many households, yet our resolve remained steadfast. We watched with suspicion as House Valerius and House Aurelian struggled, while House Marcellan and House Cassian were similarly afflicted. Our eyes turned towards House Theren, who continued to intrigue despite our efforts to uncover their secrets. Lucan Valerius attempted to expose some matters, though his attempts met with mixed success. Meanwhile, House Theren’s proposal of a union with House Solenne fell through, leaving tensions unresolved. It was during this time that a young daughter, Zoe Theren, was born, adding another thread to the tapestry of our lives. As summer approached, the air grew colder, and so did the frost upon our hearts. House Valerius again sought alliances, proposing a union with House Theren, but this too proved fruitless. Lucan Valerius confessed to House Solenne, though the outcome was uncertain. House Theren’s threats against House Corvane persisted, albeit with less intensity than before. House Marcellan’s efforts to learn the whereabouts of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor remained unfruitful, much to our dismay. Fall brought new beginnings and challenges alike. A child named Zoe Theren joined our community, bringing hope and promise. Yet, the whispers of betrayal lingered, particularly concerning House Theren. Their actions seemed calculated to undermine others, leading us to question their motives deeply. Despite these suspicions, we maintained vigilance, knowing full well the dangers lurking around us. Winter arrived, cold and unforgiving, just as our spirits felt weighed down by uncertainty. The marriage negotiations between Seraphine Solenne and Marius Corvane concluded in a betrothal, though the terms left room for doubt. House Corvane bribed Rufus Cassian, further complicating our already tense relations. Dreams foretold perilous harbors and hidden truths, urging caution and preparation. Through it all, we stood firm, guided by the ancient wisdom passed down through generations. The Severins have always believed that trust should be earned, and thus we scrutinized each action closely.

## As Basil Marcellan, merchant of the counting house on the quay, of House Marcellan wrote it

In the year of our Lord, we House Marcellan endured much hunger and suspicion. Our fortunes waxed and waned according to the whims of others, yet we remained steadfast in our ways. We saw the Valerians suffer through another spring and summer of scarcity, their hopes dashed by threats and feuds. We too were troubled when Lucan Valerius attempted to expose secrets, though his efforts met with little success. It seemed that the Valerians' troubles stemmed largely from their constant bickering among themselves, rather than any external threat. Our relations with the Aurelians were similarly strained. Their attempts to form alliances and marriages often fell flat, leaving both sides unsatisfied. However, we did manage to threaten Cassian without consequence, though their attempts to do so backfired. It was during the summer months that matters became particularly tense. House Theren sought to marry into House Solenne, but their proposal was rebuffed. Meanwhile, Lucan Valerius confided in us regarding some secret he wished to share, though the outcome was unclear. This confession added fuel to the fire already burning between Valerius and Solenne. As autumn approached, we witnessed the birth of a new life - a daughter named Zoe, born to Selene Veyra and Varro Theren. Yet despite this joy, tensions continued to simmer. House Corvane grew increasingly suspicious of House Theren, leading to several threatening exchanges. Despite these confrontations, none led to significant action. The winter months brought further challenges. House Valerius faced renewed hardships, while House Cassian struggled financially. The marriage negotiations between Seraphine Solenne and Marius Corvane concluded in a betrothal, though the terms were ambiguous. Meanwhile, House Marcellan endeavored to uncover information about Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor, who had vanished without a trace. Throughout the year, we watched closely as various factions maneuvered for power and influence. While we leaned towards House Severin due to their recent actions, we maintained a wary stance towards House Corvane. House Theren remained a source of concern, especially after their failed marriage proposals and threats. In conclusion, we House Marcellan weathered the storms of the year, maintaining our resolve and adapting to changing circumstances.
