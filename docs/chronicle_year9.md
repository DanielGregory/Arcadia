# The Chronicles of Corva: Year 9

*The world engine's sandbox (offline). Each year is written by the city's chroniclers, each from their own house: its loyalties, its grudges, its beliefs about the Flight. These are their claims, not facts, and they disagree. What actually happened is the engine's record, in the season logs.*

## As Iola Aurelian, scribe of the sea temple, of House Aurelian wrote it

In the year of the hungering winds, we saw the threads of suspicion and intrigue weave through the tapestry of Corva. Our house endured the same trials as others, but within these hardships, we discerned the signs of the divine will guiding our path. We heard whispers of betrayal among the ranks, yet we remained steadfast in our belief that Livia Corvane departed willingly, guided by love. Her departure was seen as a personal choice rather than coercion, though some whispered darkly of Soren of the Harbor’s hand in her leaving. Our envoys searched far and wide, scouring the coasts and mountainsides for traces of the missing pair. We held firm in our faith that they would return, knowing the power of the Sea's Bargain that bound our ancestors to the sacred temple. Yet, amidst the shadows cast by rivalries, there emerged a moment of unity when we united against those who sought to exploit the confusion. We defended our honor and territory, posting guards at the market to deter threats. It was during this time that a new life was born—Xanthos Marcellan, a sign perhaps of hope amid the turmoil. As winter approached, the air grew colder, and so did the tensions. Yet, even in the chill, we maintained our vigilance, ready to face whatever challenges might come. The dreams shared by our kin provided solace and guidance, offering visions of safety and protection. Through it all, we upheld the legacy of our forebears, remembering the promise made to the sea. And thus, we continued to navigate the currents of fate, ever mindful of the lessons learned and the paths yet to be trodden.

## As Aldric Severin, eldest of the judges, of House Severin wrote it

In the year of the hunger, we saw many faces turn towards suspicion and mistrust. Our house, House Severin, held fast to our belief that House Theren orchestrated the Flight to bring shame upon House Valerius. We watched with growing unease as accusations flew across the city, but they all ended in whispers and shadows. We noted with particular interest the attempts by House Corvane to uncover the truth behind the Flight. Their efforts were met with mixed success, often leading to further intrigue rather than clarity. It was clear that the seeds of discord sown during the Spring did not dissipate into autumn, nor did they find resolution in the Winter. Our spies reported that Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor remained elusive, evading capture despite relentless searches. These reports fueled our suspicions even more, for if they truly sought justice, why would they hide? It was also notable how House Valerius and House Theren engaged in heated exchanges. While House Valerius attempted to expose House Theren, these actions fell short of concrete evidence. Conversely, House Theren’s threats against House Corvane grew bolder, yet still lacked tangible proof. As the seasons turned, so too did the tensions within our city. The birth of a child, Xanthos Marcellan, brought joy to some hearts, but it also served as a reminder of the fragile state of our alliances. The dreams shared among the populace mirrored the turmoil outside—Ysolde Veyra saw the lake betrayed, while Varro Theren dreamed of his olive terraces under attack. Despite the constant vigilance and the ever-present threat of betrayal, we continued to uphold our traditions and honor our ancestors' legacy. The First Judgment remains etched in our minds—a testament to the strength of unity amidst division. Yet, the specter of doubt lingered, casting long shadows over our hopes for peace and understanding.

## As Basil Marcellan, merchant of the counting house on the quay, of House Marcellan wrote it

In the year of our Lord 1456, we House Marcellan endured another season of hunger and suspicion. Our fortunes waxed and waned according to the shifting winds of intrigue and accusation. We watched with bated breath as the whispers concerning the Flight grew louder, yet remained unconfirmed. We believed that Livia Corvane departed willingly, choosing the man she loved over the bonds of duty. Yet some within our ranks harbored doubts, whispering darkly of plots hatched by House Theren to undermine the reputation of House Valerius. These suspicions festered but yielded little concrete evidence. Our efforts to uncover the truth met with limited success. House Corvane attempted to expose House Theren’s involvement, though their attempts fell short. Similarly, House Valerius sought to implicate House Theren without success. Meanwhile, House Cassian also endeavored to reveal secrets, albeit with less pronounced results. Despite these endeavors, the enigma surrounding the Flight persisted. It became clear that many held different opinions regarding the nature of the affair. While some trusted Livia’s decision, others suspected foul play orchestrated by those close to the couple. As the seasons turned, so too did the tensions among the nobles. House Corvane continued to accuse House Aurelian, while House Valerius faced threats from both House Theren and House Cassian. Each side maneuvered carefully, ensuring alliances and maintaining feuds. Amongst our own, there emerged a new member—Xanthos Marcellan, born to Clio Valerius and Orin Marcellan. His arrival added a fresh face to our household, promising hope for future generations. Throughout the year, we maintained vigilance, ever mindful of the potential dangers lurking around us. With each passing day, the weight of expectation pressed upon us, urging us to act decisively if we wished to secure our position amidst the swirling currents of suspicion and rumor.
