# The Chronicles of Corva: Year 3

*The world engine's sandbox (offline). Each year is written by the city's chroniclers, each from their own house: its loyalties, its grudges, its beliefs about the Flight. These are their claims, not facts, and they disagree. What actually happened is the engine's record, in the season logs.*

## As Iola Aurelian, scribe of the sea temple, of House Aurelian wrote it

In the year of the winds' sighing, we saw much movement among the houses of Corva. Yet amidst these tumultuous times, it was clear that our house, House Aurelian, stood firm, guided by the ancient wisdom passed down through generations. We heard whispers of a young couple, Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor, who had vanished without a trace. This news troubled us deeply, but we held fast to the belief that Livia had chosen her path willingly. We sought answers within the walls of the sea temple, hoping to uncover the truth behind their disappearance. Our efforts yielded little, save for suspicions cast upon rivalries and threats exchanged across the houses. Our greatest concern lay with House Theren, whom we detested. Their actions towards us were often threatening, yet we managed to deflect their attempts to expose us. Despite these challenges, we remained vigilant, ensuring that our presence was felt throughout the land. As seasons changed, so too did the tales whispered around the firesides. Some spoke of Livia leaving voluntarily, while others claimed she had been abducted. These stories divided our thoughts, though we continued to uphold the faith that Livia had acted freely. This year also marked significant changes within our house. A new life entered into the fold with the birth of Valeria Valerius, daughter of Aelia Veyra and Bion. Her arrival added another thread to the tapestry of our lineage. Yet despite the turmoil, we maintained our resolve, steadfastly believing in the promise made by our founding priests centuries ago. For us, the Sea's Bargain remains etched in memory—a testament to the enduring bond between our house and the divine powers of the sea. Through trials and tribulations, we shall continue to stand tall, guided by the unwavering spirit of our ancestors.

## As Aldric Severin, eldest of the judges, of House Severin wrote it

In the spring, we saw the birth of a new life, a child named Irene Marcellan, who would grow into a future hope for our house. This event marked a time of joy and promise within our ranks. However, amidst these moments of celebration, tensions rose among the neighboring houses. We were approached by House Corvane regarding inquiries about House Theren, which led to some confusion but ultimately yielded little concrete information. Our efforts to uncover the whereabouts of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor continued throughout the summer months. Despite searching diligently, both the sea temple and the market remained empty of any sign of them. Our suspicions grew stronger towards House Theren, whom we had long distrusted, leading to several threats and counter-threats. Meanwhile, House Valerius attempted to marry into House Corvane, though their proposal fell through due to internal disagreements. As autumn arrived, the search intensified yet again. Calls for exposure echoed across the city, with various attempts to gather evidence falling short. The threat of war loomed large, particularly when House Cassian sought to confront House Theren directly. Yet, despite our best efforts, no definitive proof emerged concerning the mysterious disappearance of Livia and Soren. By the end of the year, whispers persisted about the Flight—rumors swirling around the idea that either Soren had abducted Livia or that she had fled northward. These speculations fueled suspicion and mistrust among the nobles, especially those aligned with House Theren. In the face of such uncertainty, our resolve strengthened, and we vowed to continue our vigilance until justice was served. Throughout the year, we maintained our stance against House Theren, viewing them with deep suspicion. At the same time, we held cautious optimism toward House Solenne, whose actions often seemed ambiguous and unpredictable. The coming year promised more trials and tribulations, but we stood firm, guided by the legacy of our ancestors and the unwavering belief that truth would eventually prevail.

## As Basil Marcellan, merchant of the counting house on the quay, of House Marcellan wrote it

In the autumn, we House Marcellan sought answers concerning the mysterious flight of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor. We had heard whispers among the merchants and sailors that these were the ones who had eluded capture after their supposed betrayal during the recent trials. Our efforts proved fruitless, yet we continued to search, hoping to uncover more than mere rumors. We also faced challenges within our own ranks. Calls for exposure grew louder, but none could bring forth concrete evidence. Instead, suspicions swirled around the actions of House Cassian, whom we distrusted deeply. Their threats and attempts to undermine others seemed calculated, though we could not prove any wrongdoing. Meanwhile, outside our walls, the situation remained tense. House Theren’s silence over the whereabouts of Livia and Soren added fuel to the fire, while House Valerius attempted to capitalize on the confusion. Despite our best efforts, the truth eluded us all, leaving us both frustrated and vigilant. As winter approached, the weight of uncertainty pressed heavily upon us. Yet amidst the shadows cast by the Flight, we held fast to our belief that Livia had chosen freedom willingly, guided perhaps by the hand of someone unseen. This conviction sustained us through the coming months, even if the full truth remained hidden behind veils of secrecy and suspicion.
