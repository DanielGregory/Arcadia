# The Chronicles of Corva: Year 4

*The world engine's sandbox (offline). Each year is written by the city's chroniclers, each from their own house: its loyalties, its grudges, its beliefs about the Flight. These are their claims, not facts, and they disagree. What actually happened is the engine's record, in the season logs.*

## As Iola Aurelian, scribe of the sea temple, of House Aurelian wrote it

In the year of the winds' sighing and the waves' whisper, the affairs of House Aurelian unfolded according to the will of the sea. We sought truth amidst the shadows cast by suspicion and intrigue, but often found ourselves adrift without clear answers. This year, the hunger of House Cassian persisted, a constant reminder of the trials faced by those beneath the shadow of the city. Our young ones also grew, with Lavinia coming of age under the tutelage of Ismene, while Nerva and Decimus began to take their place among the elders. Our efforts to uncover the secrets of the Flight continued, though with limited success. While some believed Livia had chosen Soren freely, others suspected foul play. Yet, despite these whispers, we remained steadfast in our belief that the founders' bargain with the sea stood firm, even if the details eluded us. Tiberon Theren's search for Livia and Soren proved fruitless once more, much to our disappointment. Meanwhile, within our own ranks, tensions simmered. House Corvane's attempts to expose the Therens met with mixed results, as did their threats towards House Theren. House Valerius, too, engaged in skirmishes with House Theren, though none led to decisive action. Silas Aurelian conducted another ritual at the sea temple, seeking guidance from the ancient waters. His actions were seen as both hopeful and foreboding, depending on whom one spoke to. In contrast, Quintus Severin's deployment of guards at the market seemed merely precautionary, yet it served to heighten the sense of unease among the populace. As seasons changed, so too did the landscape of alliances and rivalries. The marriage proposals between Houses Theren and Corvane fell short, leaving hopes unfulfilled. Similarly, the threat posed by House Damaris to House Solenne was met with counter-threats, creating a tense atmosphere throughout the city. Yet, amidst these challenges, we maintained our resolve. The birth of a child, Delia Marcellan and Silas Aurelian’s daughter, offered a moment of joy and continuity. It was a testament to the enduring strength of our lineage, even in times of uncertainty. Through it all, House Aurelian stood tall, guided by faith and tradition.

## As Aldric Severin, eldest of the judges, of House Severin wrote it

In the year of the winds' sighing, the affairs of House Severin saw much movement yet little resolution. We watched with suspicion as whispers of the Flight continued to circulate among the populace, though no concrete evidence emerged. It seemed that both hope and fear lingered within the hearts of those who sought truth amidst the shadows cast by the past. We noted with concern how House Cassian struggled through another season of scarcity, while House Valerius remained ever vigilant towards any hint of treachery. Our eyes turned towards House Theren, whose attempts to uncover secrets regarding the Flight met with varying degrees of success. Their efforts often stirred unrest amongst neighboring houses, particularly when accusations flew freely across the streets. Our spies reported that Tiberon Theren had ventured into the mountains searching for signs of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor, but returned empty-handed. This did little to quell the tension that simmered beneath the surface, especially after the failed marriage proposals between Theren and Corvane families. Meanwhile, House Severin attempted to gather information concerning the whereabouts of these mysterious individuals, posting guards at strategic locations such as the market and the mountain sheepfolds. Yet, despite our diligence, no trace could be found of either Livia or Soren, leaving us frustrated and questioning once more. As the seasons changed, so too did the dynamics of our alliances and rivalries. House Marcellan’s inquiries into Nikias Marcellan’s fate raised eyebrows, and their threats towards Rufus Cassian added fuel to the fire already burning between our two houses. Meanwhile, House Damaris’ searches for Livia and Soren proved fruitless, adding to the growing unease. Throughout the year, we observed the dreams of various inhabitants, some filled with visions of ancient cities and realms, others reflecting personal anxieties and desires. Amongst these, the recurring theme of the western gate and the city of Kentoune persisted, perhaps a harbinger of deeper truths still hidden. In conclusion, the year passed under the watchful eye of House Severin, marked by uncertainty and suspicion. While we endeavored to unravel the mysteries surrounding the Flight, the true nature of the events remained shrouded in mystery, much like the elusive figures of Livia and Soren.

## As Basil Marcellan, merchant of the counting house on the quay, of House Marcellan wrote it

In the year of our passing, the winds blew harshly across Corva, stirring up more than just the leaves of the ancient trees lining the riverbank. Our house, House Marcellan, stood firm amidst the shifting sands of suspicion and intrigue, guided by the wisdom of our forebears and the ever-watchful eyes of those around us. We saw Spring unfold with whispers of discontent among House Cassian, their hunger growing palpable under the weight of recent losses. Meanwhile, young Lavinia Valerius reached maturity, setting the stage for future alliances and rivalries. We noted with interest how House Corvane attempted to uncover secrets regarding House Theren’s role in the Flight, but these efforts met with limited success. It seemed that some truths remained hidden, even if others sought to unveil them. As Summer approached, tensions escalated further. House Corvane intensified their inquiries into House Theren, though again without concrete evidence. House Valerius continued to threaten House Corvane, while Lucan Valerius himself tried to expose the truth behind the mysterious Flight. These actions did little to ease the simmering animosity between the families. It was during Autumn when the situation became particularly tense. The marriage negotiations between Galen Theren and Marius Corvane fell through, leaving both parties disappointed and suspicious. House Theren’s search for Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor proved fruitless, adding another layer of uncertainty to the narrative. Meanwhile, House Marcellan launched a formal inquiry into Nikias Marcellan, seeking answers that would strengthen our resolve and bolster our position within the city. Winter brought a sense of finality as Selene Veyra and Varro Theren united in matrimony, bringing stability to their respective households. Yet, the mystery surrounding Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor persisted, casting long shadows over all discussions. Despite our best efforts, the trail led nowhere, leaving us questioning once more the true nature of their departure. Throughout the year, House Marcellan maintained its vigilance, always ready to strike back should any betrayal come to light. We believed firmly that Livia had chosen her path willingly, yet doubts lingered among some quarters. This belief fortified our stance against House Theren, whom we viewed with deep mistrust.
