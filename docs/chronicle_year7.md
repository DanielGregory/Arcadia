# The Chronicles of Corva: Year 7

*The world engine's sandbox (offline). Each year is written by the city's chroniclers, each from their own house: its loyalties, its grudges, its beliefs about the Flight. These are their claims, not facts, and they disagree. What actually happened is the engine's record, in the season logs.*

## As Iola Aurelian, scribe of the sea temple, of House Aurelian wrote it

In the year of the winds' sighing and the waves' murmur, we, the faithful servants of the sea, endured hunger and strife. Yet through it all, our resolve remained steadfast, guided by the ancient teachings of the Temple. Spring saw our brethren go hungry, but within these walls, we held a sacred ritual under the watchful eyes of the sea. It was a time of prayer and contemplation, seeking guidance from the divine. We also learned of threats and intrigues from neighboring houses, yet we stood firm, ready to defend our sanctuary and honor. As summer approached, tensions rose higher. House Valerius sought alliances while others plotted against them. Our own efforts to uncover the truth behind the mysterious disappearance of Livia and Soren met with varied success. Despite these challenges, we continued to seek answers, believing firmly in the sanctity of our cause. During the autumn months, whispers grew louder about the fate of our missing companions. While some suspected foul play, others maintained hope that they had chosen freedom together. This uncertainty did little to ease our hearts, though we pressed forward with determination. By the end of the year, winter brought more trials. Houses Theren and Corvane engaged in heated disputes over the whereabouts of Livia and Soren. Each side sent search parties across the lands and seas, leaving many unanswered questions. Amidst these conflicts, we remembered the lessons of our ancestors and vowed to uphold the integrity of our faith. Through it all, we remain vigilant, ever mindful of the Sea's Bargain. For us, the true test lies ahead, as we await the return of those who have gone astray. Until then, let us pray and prepare ourselves for whatever comes next.

## As Aldric Severin, eldest of the judges, of House Severin wrote it

In the year of the hungering winds, we of House Severin saw much movement among the houses, but little resolution. We were ever vigilant, guarding the markets and seeking whispers of those who sought to undermine the peace. We heard tales of threats and alliances, yet they seemed more hollow than solid. House Valerius and House Aurelian struggled through another season of scarcity, while House Marcellan and House Cassian faced similar trials. Yet these struggles did not bring clarity to the matters concerning the Flight. Our suspicions grew towards House Theren, whom we had long distrusted. Their actions this year furthered our belief that they played some role in the mysterious disappearance of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor. Our efforts to uncover their involvement met with mixed success, though we continued to post guards at the market, hoping to catch them in their lies. Meanwhile, House Corvane attempted to expose the truth behind the Flight, but their attempts fell short. This led to accusations and counter-accusations, leaving many questions unanswered. It became clear that the Flight remained shrouded in mystery, and no single explanation could satisfy all parties involved. As the seasons turned, so too did the tensions within our city. House Valerius and House Aurelian continued to face challenges, while House Marcellan and House Cassian also endured hardships. These difficulties provided fertile ground for suspicion and intrigue, but ultimately failed to resolve the central issue of the Flight. Through it all, we stood firm, guided by the ancient wisdom passed down through generations. We believed that the Flight must have been orchestrated by someone outside our midst, and we would not rest until the truth was revealed. For now, the shadows loomed large, and the path forward remained uncertain.

## As Basil Marcellan, merchant of the counting house on the quay, of House Marcellan wrote it

In the year of the shadowed winds, we House Marcellan faced trials both within and without. Spring saw our fortunes dip, as did those of many others. Yet amidst these lean times, whispers began to circulate among the merchants and nobles alike—whispers of a flight, a departure so sudden and mysterious that even now, after much searching, none could pinpoint their exact whereabouts. Lysandra Marcellan’s coming of age marked another milestone, though it seemed less significant than usual under the weight of shared hunger. Our efforts to uncover the truth behind the disappearance of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor were met with mixed success. We suspected treachery from House Theren, who had been known to conspire against us, but their involvement remained unproven despite our persistent inquiries. As summer approached, tensions rose further. House Valerius’ proposal of an alliance to House Corvane fell flat, while threats and accusations flew freely across the streets. House Aurelian’s ritual at the sea temple hinted at deeper currents beneath the surface, yet yielded little concrete information. Meanwhile, House Corvane attempted to expose House Theren’s role in the flight, only to have their attempts rebuffed. By autumn, the search continued unabated. House Marcellan sought answers through petitions and alliances, while House Cassian pressed harder for exposure. Despite these efforts, no clear path emerged towards understanding the true nature of the flight. It became increasingly apparent that the mystery surrounding Livia and Soren would remain unsolved until they returned—or until the shadows themselves revealed more light. Winter brought no respite. Threats and counter-threats echoed through the halls and markets, leaving us ever vigilant. House Marcellan stood firm, leaning towards House Severin over the course of the year. Doubts lingered, however, particularly regarding House Theren’s potential complicity. Yet, as always, we trusted in the wisdom of our ancestors and the legacy of the Second Ship, which had brought prosperity to our city. Through it all, we maintained our resolve, hoping that the next turn of the tide might bring clarity to the enigma that consumed our thoughts and dreams. For in the end, it was the legends and the hopes of our forebears that guided us through the mists of time and uncertainty.
