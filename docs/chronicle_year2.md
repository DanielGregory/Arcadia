# The Chronicles of Corva: Year 2

*The world engine's sandbox (offline). Each year is written by the city's chroniclers, each from their own house: its loyalties, its grudges, its beliefs about the Flight. These are their claims, not facts, and they disagree. What actually happened is the engine's record, in the season logs.*

## As Iola Aurelian, scribe of the sea temple, of House Aurelian wrote it

In the year of our Lord Aurelius, we see many events transpire within the realm of Corva. Spring brings joyous news when Aelia Veyra and Bion were united in matrimony, yet the passing of Selene Damaris leaves a somber note among the populace. Our inquiries into House Theren continue, though none have been successful thus far. Threats and counter-threats abound, but little comes to fruition. As summer progresses, the winds shift slightly. Clio Valerius and Orin Marcellan’s union adds another thread to the tapestry of alliances. Meanwhile, House Aurelian seeks answers regarding the whereabouts of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor, who remain elusive despite our efforts. We must remember the ancient prophecy of the Sea's Bargain, which binds our fate closely to the whims of the ocean. Fall brings more challenges. House Corvane faces threats from various quarters, including attempts to uncover secrets involving Livia and Soren. Despite these difficulties, our resolve remains steadfast. Winter arrives, bringing new marriages—Zoe and Titus join together—and further complications arise as suspicions grow around the mysterious disappearance of Livia and Soren. Our belief—that Livia left willingly—is bolstered by whispers and dreams. Yet some doubt persists, questioning if force was used. Dreams speak of storms and shadows, hinting at hidden truths beneath the surface. In the end, the truth remains veiled, much like the enigmatic journey of Livia and Soren across the vast expanse of the sea.

## As Aldric Severin, eldest of the judges, of House Severin wrote it

In the year of the sun’s slow descent into winter, we, House Severin, observed much activity within our borders and beyond. We noted the union of Clio Valerius and Orin Marcellan, which seemed to bring some measure of stability to House Valerius. However, tensions rose when House Corvane sought alliances with both House Theren and House Valerius, actions that did little to quell suspicion among our neighbors. Our efforts to uncover the whereabouts of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor continued, though all searches proved fruitless. This lack of success led to increased scrutiny towards House Theren, who remained elusive despite repeated inquiries. Our spies reported that Tiberon Theren had ventured into the mountains searching for these individuals but returned empty-handed. We also witnessed the wedding of Laurent Veyra and Ione Marcellan, adding another couple to the tapestry of our kinship network. Yet, amidst these personal matters, the larger question of the Flight loomed large. Many believed that House Theren orchestrated the departure to undermine House Valerius, while others suspected forceful abduction. Despite our mistrust of House Theren, we could not ignore the whispers of those who claimed Soren of the Harbor spirited away his wife against her will. As winter approached, so too did the chill of doubt surrounding the mysterious disappearance of Livia and Soren. Their absence cast shadows upon our plans and ambitions, prompting further investigations and threats. It became clear that neither House Solenne nor House Theren would yield any information easily, leaving us to wonder if the truth lay hidden behind the veil of secrecy. Through the lens of our records, we see a year marked by intrigue and uncertainty, yet steadfast determination to unravel the mystery of the Flight remains central to our resolve. In the coming months, we shall continue to probe deeper, seeking answers that might restore order to our once-peaceful city.

## As Basil Marcellan, merchant of the counting house on the quay, of House Marcellan wrote it

In the year of our Lord 1457, we House Marcellan observed much activity within the city of Corva. We noted the marriages of Clio Valerius and Orin Marcellan, which strengthened alliances but did little to alter the political landscape significantly. Our efforts to uncover information regarding Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor continued without success, though we suspected that both Houses Theren and Cassian had been involved in some capacity. We also witnessed the rise of prominent figures such as Calla Aurelian and Aulus Damaris, who seemed to gain influence despite our attempts to undermine them. It was during this time that Nikias Marcellan presented a petition to the council, seeking support for his cause, yet it met with mixed responses. Meanwhile, House Severin attempted to expose the truth behind the mysterious flight of Livia and Soren, but their efforts fell short. Our dealings with Rufus Cassian remained contentious, leading to several bribes and threats exchanged. Despite these tensions, we managed to secure a favorable outcome once again, albeit with lingering suspicions. The winter months brought more dreams, including those of Dion, Lysandra Veyra, and Phaedra Severin, none of which provided clear insights into the whereabouts of Livia and Soren. As the year drew to a close, we reflected on the ongoing mystery surrounding the Flight. While many believed Livia had chosen freedom willingly, others speculated that House Theren might have orchestrated the escape to humiliate House Valerius. This speculation fueled suspicion towards House Theren, whom we already distrusted due to past conflicts. We leaned towards House Severin, viewing them as potential allies in unraveling the enigma of the Flight. In conclusion, while the year was marked by intrigue and conflict, our focus remained steadfastly upon maintaining our position and securing any advantage possible. The true nature of Livia’s departure remains uncertain, leaving us ever vigilant and prepared for whatever challenges lie ahead.
