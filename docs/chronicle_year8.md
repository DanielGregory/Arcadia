# The Chronicles of Corva: Year 8

*The world engine's sandbox (offline). Each year is written by the city's chroniclers, each from their own house: its loyalties, its grudges, its beliefs about the Flight. These are their claims, not facts, and they disagree. What actually happened is the engine's record, in the season logs.*

## As Iola Aurelian, scribe of the sea temple, of House Aurelian wrote it

In the year of the winds' sighing and the waves' murmur, we, the faithful servants of the Sea Temple, faced trials both within and without. Our hearts grew heavy with hunger, yet through the storms, we stood firm, guided by the ancient wisdom passed down through generations. We heard whispers of betrayal among our neighbors, but none could confirm the truth behind the mysterious disappearance of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor. Their absence cast a shadow over our hopes and dreams, causing unease amongst many. Our enemies, notably House Theren, stirred unrest, threatening alliances and bribes. Yet, despite these challenges, we remained vigilant, ever mindful of the Sea's bargain and the sacred duty bestowed upon us by the founding priests. Nerva Corvane's threats towards House Theren seemed to have little effect, while his actions towards House Veyra revealed a complex web of intrigue. Meanwhile, House Corvane continued to pay bribes, attempting to sway the balance of power. Silas Aurelian sought answers regarding Livia and Soren, though he too found himself thwarted. His efforts did not bring clarity, leaving us more uncertain than ever. As seasons changed, so did the landscape of suspicion and fear. We watched as House Aurelian proposed a union with House Solenne, hoping to strengthen our position amidst shifting allegiances. However, such plans met resistance from those who believed Livia had chosen freedom. Through it all, we held fast to our traditions and the belief that Livia's departure was voluntary. Her choice, however painful, was hers alone, and we must honor her decision, even if it leaves us questioning the path ahead. This year has been fraught with uncertainty, but we remain steadfast, ready to face whatever comes next under the watchful eye of the sea. For in the end, it is the will of the sea that guides us, and we shall follow its lead until the day when the true purpose of our existence becomes clear once more.

## As Aldric Severin, eldest of the judges, of House Severin wrote it

In the year of the gathering shadows, we, House Severin, faced trials both within and beyond our walls. Spring brought hunger to all but our own, yet it was during these lean times that alliances began to form and dissolve. We heard whispers of intrigue among the neighboring houses, particularly concerning the mysterious disappearance of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor. Our suspicions fell upon House Theren, who sought to undermine the Valerians through subtle means, while others whispered of abduction and betrayal. As summer approached, the air grew thick with tension. House Corvane attempted to secure favors through bribery, attempting to sway matters in their favor. Yet, despite their efforts, the truth remained elusive. It was during this time that Quintus Severin placed sentries at the market, his vigilance a testament to our unwavering commitment to justice and protection. Fall brought further challenges. House Aurelian continued to suffer under the weight of scarcity, and rumors swirled around the fate of our missing allies. Despite our best efforts, the trail led nowhere. Meanwhile, House Theren’s proposal for a union with House Solenne met with mixed reactions, neither gaining nor losing ground significantly. Winter deepened into a season of suspicion and fear. House Corvane again turned to bribes, hoping to influence outcomes. However, their attempts failed to yield any concrete evidence. The search for Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor intensified, leading some to question the veracity of their claims and motives. Throughout the year, we maintained our stance—distrustful of those who would seek to disrupt the harmony of our society. House Severin believed firmly that House Theren orchestrated the supposed flight to humiliate House Valerius, though many doubted such bold actions could have gone unnoticed. Others speculated more sinisterly, suggesting force had been used against Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor. Our dreams provided glimpses into the unknown. Ysolde Veyra dreamed of mountains shrouded in mystery, hinting at hidden truths. Drusilla Valerius saw a golden figure adorned with precious metals, perhaps symbolizing wealth and power. These visions added layers to our understanding of what transpired, though they did little to clarify the enigma surrounding our missing companions.

## As Basil Marcellan, merchant of the counting house on the quay, of House Marcellan wrote it

In the year of our Lord 1457, we House Marcellan faced many trials and tribulations. Spring brought hunger to all but our own, yet it also marked significant inquiries into matters concerning our enemies. We heard whispers of a potential alliance between House Theren and House Solenne, though these efforts proved fruitless. Our own inquiries led us to uncover some intriguing details regarding the movements of Livia Corvane and Soren of the Harbor, who had gone missing during the previous year. Despite searching diligently, both Ysolde Veyra and Varro Damaris failed to locate any trace of them. As the seasons turned, so did the tensions within our city. Summer brought more threats and counter-threats among the nobles, particularly towards House Corvane. It seemed that everyone wanted to lay claim to the mysterious disappearance of Livia and Soren. However, despite their best efforts, none could provide concrete evidence or bring them back. Fall continued to be a time of uncertainty and suspicion. House Aurelian attempted to form a union with House Solenne, while House Theren again sought to align themselves with House Solenne, once more without success. Meanwhile, our own attempts to understand the flight remained inconclusive, leading to further mistrust and speculation amongst the populace. Winter brought even greater challenges. House Corvane grew increasingly agitated, threatening others and attempting to uncover the truth behind the Flight. Yet, despite their efforts, no clear answers emerged. The dreams reported by those around us added another layer of mystery, leaving much unexplained. Throughout the year, we House Marcellan held fast to our belief that Livia Corvane had chosen freedom over duty, choosing Soren of the Harbor rather than her betrothal to Lucan Valerius. This view aligned closely with our suspicions of House Theren’s involvement, whom we viewed with increasing distrust. While we leaned towards House Severin, our alliances remained tenuous due to ongoing feuds and rivalries. Our chronicler records these events faithfully, noting how the threads of intrigue and betrayal weave through the fabric of our society, shaping destinies and altering the course of our ancient city.
