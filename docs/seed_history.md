=== 1. THE FLIGHT FROM CORVA (Danny's seed story) ===

Public account: what Corva knows or believes

Livia Corvane, aged twenty, was the only daughter of Marius Corvane, the aging lord of House Corvane. Clever, spirited, and unwilling to be treated as a bargaining piece, she had long resented the marriage arranged for her with Lucan Valerius, the twenty-six-year-old heir of House Valerius. Lucan was neither cruel nor dishonorable. He was a proud and disciplined man who believed deeply in duty, and he considered the marriage a necessary union between two great houses.

Soren of the Harbor, aged twenty-two, was born a bastard. He never knew his father, and his mother died when he was young. His birth left him without a family name, and the city knew him by the place where he had been found. Galen Theren, head of House Theren, took him in as a child and raised him alongside his own sons, giving him a place in the household. Soren grew into a trusted harbor pilot and sailor, known for his courage, quick wit, and stubborn loyalty. Though the Therens treated him as one of their own, he never entirely escaped the judgment of his birth. Galen's eldest son, Tiberon, never accepted him as a brother.

Livia and Soren met four years earlier during a summer gathering at the harbor. Occasional conversations became a secret courtship, carried on through garden meetings, quiet walks along the coast, and letters delivered by servants.

They fled during the autumn festival of the sea, when much of Corva was gathered along the harbor for music, feasting, and religious ceremonies. Livia took only a small traveling cloak, a purse of coins, and her mother's gold ring, an heirloom of House Corvane. An old temple servant later claimed to have seen two figures leaving through the western gate.

By sunrise, the lovers were gone.

Rumors spread through Corva before the morning markets opened. Some claimed Soren had kidnapped Livia. Others insisted she had abandoned her house willingly. A few whispered that House Theren had arranged the escape to humiliate Valerius and weaken its influence. One sailor swore he had seen their vessel sailing east, while a merchant claimed they had taken the northern road.

Marius Corvane demanded his daughter be returned, though whether from grief, anger, or fear of disgrace, none could say. House Valerius demanded satisfaction for the insult to its heir and the breaking of the betrothal. House Theren faced an impossible choice: protect the young man it had raised as family, or preserve the peace that had protected the house for generations. House Damaris feared that one of its servants knew more than she was saying.

Three nights before the festival, an old priestess had dreamed of a golden ring sinking beneath dark water while two stars disappeared from the heavens. She told the temple attendants, but they dismissed it as the ramblings of an old woman. Now people had begun asking what else she might have seen.

Where Livia and Soren had gone, no one in Corva knew.
