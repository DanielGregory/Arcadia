They say that in the hills above Corva there should be a mark for years before they are all loosed in their order to sail towards the other world. And that they were informed that the great sun was going onwards and that the land, which was so much constrained by this scowdowd and sent down the sea into water, was to be found. The stones ceased to shake and extinguished the ship to the right foot, which made the sails of them as it had been hot. And when the Athenians saw the Agesians so well off, and finding this, they ran up into the open sea, leaving the territory and founded most of the ships sailing into the country. And having perceived, the Peloponnesians seeing no one to be able to retreat, although I did not escape from those who came near the Hellespont.
